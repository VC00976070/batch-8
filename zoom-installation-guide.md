## Overview

Zoom is a video conferencing tool used for online meetings, webinars, and virtual collaboration. This document provides instructions for installing, setting up, and using the Zoom Desktop Client on supported operating systems.

## Prerequisites

Before you install Zoom, make sure that you have:

- Access to a reliable internet connection
- Administrator rights on your device
- A compatible operating system

## System requirements

**Table 1. System requirements**

| Component | Minimum requirement           | Sample |
| -----:    | :------------------------------:| ------: |
| Processor | Dual-core 2.0 GHz or higher   | 1 |
| RAM       | 4 GB                          | 2 |
| Operating system | Windows 10/11, macOS 10.13+ | 3 |

## Install Zoom Desktop Client

1. Open your browser and go to [zoom.us/download](https://zoom.us/download).
1. Locate the Zoom Desktop Client section and click Download.
    > **Tip:** This saves the installer file to your device.
1. Run the installer:
    - On Windows: Double-click `ZoomInstaller.exe`.
    - On macOS: Double-click `Zoom.pkg`.
1. Follow the setup wizard.
1. Accept the `icense terms`
1. Choose the directory `here`you want to install Zoom.
1. Click **Install** and wait for the installation to complete.
1. Click **Finish** or **Close** when the installation is complete.
1. Open Zoom from your desktop or Applications folder.

## Create an account or sign in

Launch the Zoom app or visit [zoom.us/signup](https://zoom.us/signup).

### Create an account

1. Enter your email address and verify your age.
1. Check your inbox for a confirmation email.
1. Click the activation link to proceed.

### Sign in

Use your Zoom credentials to sign in at [zoom.us/signin](https://zoom.us/signin).

> **Tip:** You can also sign in with Google, Facebook, or SSO.
